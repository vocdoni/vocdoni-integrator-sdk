`vote()` now skips the questions the voter is not eligible for (`canVote: false` in the CSP check, when the census is split into per-question eligibility subsets). They are never encoded, signed or relayed, so the CSP no longer refuses the whole sign batch over one ballot, and apps can pass `[]` for them instead of a placeholder answer. `voteStatus` gets `'ineligible'` for them, set on connect and not once the process has ended (nor in the last five minutes before its end date, when a voter with no votable question is not marked at all, since device clocks drift). `QuestionVoteStatus` gains that member, so an exhaustive `switch` over it needs a new case. A question the voter already voted or signed keeps its status. A secret question without published `encryptionKeys` blocks `vote()` only when this call would sign it, so an ineligible one no longer does. `vote()` throws the new `NoEligibleQuestionError` (`questions`) before signing anything when every question is on chain or ineligible and at least one is ineligible. It re-checks eligibility on each call: a question that read `ineligible` and now reads `canVote: true`, passed an empty ballot, makes `vote()` throw the new `EligibilityChangedError` (`questions`) before signing anything.

`<ElectionQuestions />` shows the `vote.disabled.question_ineligible` notice on such a question, renders no fields for it, requires no answer and leaves it out of the confirmation. `describeVoteError` maps `NoEligibleQuestionError` to the new `errors.vote_ineligible_question` and `errors.vote_ineligible_questions` keys, and `EligibilityChangedError` to `errors.vote_eligibility_changed`.
