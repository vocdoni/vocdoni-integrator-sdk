`vote()` now skips the questions the voter is not eligible for (`canVote: false` in the CSP check, when the census is split into per-question eligibility subsets). They are never encoded, signed or relayed, so the CSP no longer refuses the whole sign batch over one ballot, and apps can pass `[]` for them instead of a placeholder answer. `voteStatus` gets `'ineligible'` for them, set on connect and not once the process has ended (nor in the last five minutes before its end date, when a voter with no votable question is not marked at all, since device clocks drift). `QuestionVoteStatus` gains that member, so an exhaustive `switch` over it needs a new case. A question the voter already voted or signed keeps its status. A secret question without published `encryptionKeys` blocks `vote()` only when this call would sign it, so an ineligible one no longer does.

`hasVoted` is now true once every question is on chain or ineligible and at least one is on chain, so a voter with one ineligible question sees the voted view and an app keyed on `hasVoted` shows its success screen. Before, it needed every question on chain. After the process ends the check no longer tells an ineligible question from an unvoted one, so a session opened after the end reads `hasVoted` false for such a voter. A voter with nothing on chain and every question ineligible reads `hasVoted` false, and `vote()` throws the new `NoEligibleQuestionError` (`questions`) before signing anything. Once `hasVoted` is true, `vote()` throws the existing already-voted error.

`vote()` re-checks eligibility on each call, and throws the new `EligibilityChangedError` (`questions`) before signing anything in both directions: a question that read `ineligible` and is eligible now, passed an empty ballot, and a question shown eligible that is ineligible now, passed a ballot. The form gets the fresh state first, so the question reads `ineligible` on the retry.

`<ElectionQuestions />` shows the `vote.disabled.question_ineligible` notice on such a question, renders no fields for it, requires no answer and leaves it out of the confirmation. `<VoteButton />` gives the new `vote.disabled.ineligible` reason ("You are not eligible to vote on any question.") when the voter is in the census but eligible for no question. `describeVoteError` maps `NoEligibleQuestionError` to the new `errors.vote_ineligible_question` and `errors.vote_ineligible_questions` keys, and `EligibilityChangedError` to `errors.vote_eligibility_changed`.
