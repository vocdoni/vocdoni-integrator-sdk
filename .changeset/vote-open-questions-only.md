`vote()` now signs and relays only the questions that are open (`ONGOING`), leaving paused, upcoming or ended ones for a later call, so a voter can vote the open question of a question-by-question process (#111). `isAbleToVote` is false when no unvoted question is open, and `vote()` throws the new `NoOpenQuestionError` in that case. `voteStatus` gets no entry for a question `vote()` skips.

`<ElectionQuestions />` locks a question that is not open while the process is, or is between two questions (`PROCESS_UNKNOWN`, e.g. one `ENDED` and the next `PAUSED`) (`vote.disabled.question_not_open`, also when its stored tx is pending: it is re-sent only once the question reopens) and a question already voted (`vote.disabled.question_voted`): they need no answer, get a placeholder ballot and are left out of the confirmation (new `skip` prop of `QuestionsConfirmation`). `lockedQuestionNotice` takes an optional `{ questionStatus, processStatus, voted }` (`voted`: the voter state's `hasVoted`, so a question voted in an earlier session stays locked). `<VoteButton />` explains a disabled button while no open question is left and the next one the voter can vote (`canVote`) is paused or upcoming, also between two questions (`vote.disabled.waiting_question` once the voter has voted a question, `vote.disabled.waiting_question_none` otherwise), and reads `vote.disabled.voted` when the voter voted the open questions and only ended ones are left. A `NoOpenQuestionError` reads by its `questions` (each with its own status): `errors.vote_no_open_question` for one question not open yet, `errors.vote_no_open_questions` for several, and `errors.vote_closed_question` / `errors.vote_closed_questions` when they have all ended or been canceled, since those never open again. A locked question with no inferable ballot type no longer blocks the vote on the open ones.

`QuestionsConfirmationAnswerItem` gains `index` (its position in `election.questions`) and `questionId`, because `answersView` leaves out the skipped questions. `lockedQuestionNotice` treats a raw `READY` question status as `ONGOING`, and an absent one does not lock by status.
